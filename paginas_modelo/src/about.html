About Needles & VU

This site is about vintage recording equipment. We keep tape machines, classic microphones, and consoles running so people can hear music the way it was captured decades ago.

How It Started

It began with one reel-to-reel deck rescued from a radio station. From there we collected more machines, parts, and stories from engineers who used them.

Why We Care

Analog gear adds a gentle warmth and character. We maintain it so students can learn how to align tape, clean heads, and appreciate careful listening.
