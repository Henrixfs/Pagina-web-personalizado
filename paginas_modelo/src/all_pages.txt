  <h1>About Needles &amp; VU</h1>
  <p>This site is about vintage recording equipment. We keep tape machines, classic microphones, and consoles running so people can hear music the way it was captured decades ago.</p>

    <li><a href="index.html">Home</a></li>

  <h2>How It Started</h2>
  <p>It began with one reel-to-reel deck rescued from a radio station. From there we collected more machines, parts, and stories from engineers who used them.</p>

  <h2>Why We Care</h2>
  <p>Analog gear adds a gentle warmth and character. We maintain it so students can learn how to align tape, clean heads, and appreciate careful listening.</p>
